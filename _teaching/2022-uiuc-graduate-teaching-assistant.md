**General Ecology laboratory (IB 203)**
- Total credit hours taught: 2 (1 credit hour per course, 1 semester)
- Responsible for teaching fundamentals of ecology to undergraduate students

**Insect Ecology (IB 444)**
- Total credit hours taught: 4 (4 credit hours per course, 1 semester)
- Responsible for leading lab sections, grading lab reports and lecture exams, and assisting in lectures.

**Insect Classification and Evolution (IB 468)**
- Total credit hours taught: 4 (4 credit hours per course, 1 semester)
- Established integral knowledge of insect systematics, ecology, and evolution in undergraduate and graduate students.
- Developed lectures and lab materials conducive to understanding arthropod biodiversity.
