Research experience
======
* **2025–2026:** Big Ten Academic Alliance/Smithsonian Institution Predoctoral Fellow, National Museum of Natural History, Smithsonian Institution
* **2021–Present:** Graduate Research Assistant, Illinois Natural History Survey, Prairie Research Institute
  * Created and analyzed species distribution models of the midwestern stonefly fauna using Maximum Entropy and applied current phylogeographic techniques to the winter stonefly genus *Allocapnia* (Claassen, 1928).
* **2019–2021:** Graduate Research Assistant, Western Kentucky University, Center for Biodiversity Studies
  * Generated and analyzed species distribution models to test patterns of occurrence for USGS Species of Greatest Conservation Need listed stoneflies within the montane region of Maryland. Subsequent sampling from the spring and summer of 2020 resulted in new state records and the identification of new populations of rare and threatened species.
* **2018:** Research Technician, Frostburg State University
  * Archeological surveying and excavation of the abandoned coal mining town of Kempton, Maryland.
* **2016–2018:** Research Technician, Frostburg State University
  * Participated in a mark-recapture study of adult dragonflies with additional sampling of aquatic larvae. These data were prepared for a poster presentation investigating environmental influences on dragonfly community structures in Western Maryland wetlands.
* **2015–2017:** Research Technician, Frostburg State University
  * Paid position to identify larval aquatic macroinvertebrates within New Jersey wetlands to quantify waterfowl food production.
